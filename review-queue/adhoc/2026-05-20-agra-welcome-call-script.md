# Wiom Welcome Call Script — Agra
**Version:** 2.0
**Date:** 2026-05-20
**Status:** Pending human approval before deployment
**Classification:** Promotional
**Channel:** Outbound voice call — call centre agent
**Trigger:** Within 24–48 hours of new installation
**Language:** Hindi
**Audience:** New Wiom customers in Agra (U2 / end customer)
**Timing restriction:** 09:00–21:00 IST only (promotional classification)

---

## Objective

Orient the newly installed Wiom customer to the PayG model by:
1. Establishing that Wiom home internet works on recharge logic — not monthly subscription
2. Explaining the 2-day free internet period clearly (what it is, when it ends, what happens next)
3. Leaving them confident and equipped to recharge independently via the app

The call should leave the customer with one clear mental model: **recharge wala ghar ka net** — the same logic they already use for mobile, now for home internet.

---

## Call Paths

This script has two paths. The agent follows Path A (welcome) by default. If the customer reports internet not working at any point, the agent switches to Path B (escalation) immediately.

| Situation | Path |
|---|---|
| Internet is working | Path A — Welcome & Orientation |
| Internet is not working | Path B — Escalation |

---

## PATH A — WELCOME & ORIENTATION

> **AGENT — READ BEFORE DIALLING**
>
> **Why this call exists:**
> This customer just had Wiom installed. They have 2 days of free internet running. They may not yet understand how Wiom works — many will assume it is a monthly plan like Jio or Airtel.
>
> **What this call must achieve:**
> Leave the customer with one clear idea — Wiom home internet works on recharge, exactly like their mobile. They should know: (1) the 2-day free period will end, (2) they recharge through the app when they need internet, (3) they are in control — no fixed bill, no automatic deduction.
>
> **What success looks like:**
> The customer hangs up knowing how to recharge and confident they won't be caught off guard when the free period ends.

## Scope Limits for Agents

**Agents may answer:**
- How to find and download the Wiom app
- What the 2-day free period is and when it ends
- How to navigate to Recharge in the app chat
- General description of plan durations (1-day, 7-day, 28-day)

**Agents must redirect to app or helpline:**
- Specific plan prices → "App mein dikh jaate hain"
- Network coverage or speed queries → helpline
- Billing or payment issues → helpline
- Any complaint or service issue → helpline or escalate per SOP

**Agents must never:**
- Quote prices without confirmed rate card in front of them
- Promise specific resolution times for internet issues
- Improvise on policy, network, or billing questions
